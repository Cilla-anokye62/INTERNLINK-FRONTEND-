# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

InternLink

## Short description

Discover internships, apply, and track every opportunity in one place.

## Full description

InternLink brings students, employers, and universities together in one internship platform.

Students can build a career profile, discover relevant internships, save opportunities, submit applications, track progress, message employers, and receive application updates.

Employers can publish internships, review applicants, manage hiring stages, schedule interviews, send offers, and monitor listing performance.

Universities can follow student placement progress, review employer engagement, and use placement analytics to support their students.

Key features include:

- Personalized internship discovery and match recommendations
- Search, filters, saved opportunities, and application tracking
- Resume and supporting-document uploads
- Secure messaging, offers, and notifications
- Student, employer, and university dashboards
- Profile, privacy, notification, language, and appearance settings
- Account data export and deletion controls

Some capabilities, including AI recommendations and push delivery, depend on the services enabled by the organization operating InternLink.
